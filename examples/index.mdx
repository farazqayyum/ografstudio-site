Every example below is made of ordinary, editable Studio layers, so you can take it apart and make
it your own. Each example page plays the real exported OGraf package in your browser: take it in
and out, and change its data to see it update. The dark checker shows through wherever a graphic is
transparent, and the swatches under each player swap it for a colour, your webcam or an image of your own. The recipes are
built into Studio: choose them from **+ Recipe…** above the canvas.

These examples were made with the latest development build of OGraf Studio, 0.23.0-rc.1.

description.
