A leaderboard for sport and elections. Press **Next step** and the package moves to its next round:
every row keeps its identity and slides to its new position.

## How it's built

- **Runtime collection:** one row is designed once, then repeated for each entry in the `standings`
  array. Rows are keyed by `id` and sorted by `points`.
- **Steps:** the graphic has three steps, so a controller's Next button walks through them.
- **Rules:** each step fires a rule that sets the round's standings inside the graphic.
- **Fields:** the title and the full standings array, which a data feed can replace at any time.

## Make it yours

- Replace the rounds in the **Table panel** layer's rules, or drive `standings` from your own feed.
- Change `sortPath` to rank by another value, or set `pageSize` to page through a longer table.
