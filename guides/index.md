Each guide takes one task from start to finish. They follow the latest stable release; the
[documentation](/docs) has the complete reference.

## Getting started

- [Install and run OGraf Studio](/guides/install): download the single-file server, or run from
  source.
- [Make your first lower third](/guides/first-lower-third): start from a broadcast recipe, edit it,
  preview it and save it.

## Building graphics

- [Animate with steps and keyframes](/guides/animate): the Start, Step and End lifecycle, the
  timeline and Auto-keyframe.
- [Connect graphics to operator data](/guides/bind-data): add data fields and bind text, images and
  colours to them.
- [Add images, SVG and Lottie](/guides/images-svg-lottie): bring artwork in and keep it editable.
- [Create a shader background](/guides/shader-background): GLSL fills with operator controls.

## Delivering graphics

- [Export and test an OGraf package](/guides/export): produce a checked `.ograf.zip` and test it in
  your player.
- [Share projects from GitHub](/guides/share-from-github): open `.ogs` projects straight from a URL.

## Working with AI

- [Connect an AI assistant](/guides/ai-assistant): use the built-in AI Assistant, or drive Studio from
  an MCP client such as Claude or Codex.
