OGraf Studio now has a home of its own. ografstudio.org brings together everything you need to
design, animate and deliver EBU OGraf graphics with Studio.

<!-- truncate -->

## What you'll find here

- **[How-to guides](/guides)** take one task from start to finish: installing Studio, building a
  first lower third, connecting operator data, and exporting a checked package for playout.
- **[Examples](/examples)** show ready-made graphics you can open, study and adapt.
- **[Documentation](/docs)** is the complete reference, kept in step with the latest stable
  release.
- **[What's new](/releases)** has the release notes for every version since 0.01, with RSS and
  Atom feeds.
- **Announcements**, this page, is where project news appears.

## Get involved

OGraf Studio is open source under the GNU AGPL v3.0. The
[repository](https://github.com/zerodensity/ograf-studio) is the place for bug reports, feature
requests and contributions; contributions target the `dev` branch.

To try Studio, [download the latest release](https://github.com/zerodensity/ograf-studio/releases/latest)
and follow the [install guide](/guides/install).
