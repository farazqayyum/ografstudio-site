OGraf Studio 0.30 is out. It brings together the work since 0.22 and the 0.23 release candidates:
a simpler way to keyframe, a plugin gallery, shared design tools and richer previews.

<!-- truncate -->

## Keyframes, one property at a time

New objects now start with a static pose. To animate one, click the diamond beside a property in
**Properties**, or use **Key transform** in **Timeline** to record the whole transform.
Auto-keyframe is no longer needed for this. **At playhead** and **Whole animation** make it clear
what an edit will change.

**Keys** and **Graph** are now separate views that share a ruler, zoom and scroll. You can also edit
text directly on the canvas, and text presets add typewriter, rolling digits and numeric count-up or
count-down.

New to this? [Your first keyframe animation](/docs/user-guide/animation/first-keyframe-animation)
takes four steps.

## Plugins and charts

The **Plugins** gallery manages versioned `.ogsplugin.zip` packs of chart renderers, animation
presets and shaders. Applied motion becomes editable keys, shader presets become independent copies,
and existing charts keep the exact renderer they were made with.

Chart.js charts are chosen visually, take editable or data-bound values and animate when their data
updates. Supported marks can use gradients, shaders or media.

## Media, sound and Rules

Objects and backgrounds can be painted with prerecorded media or named live inputs, and a webcam can
stand in for a live input while you preview. Place **Sound Events** on the Timeline, or use **Media
Cues** for trim, loop, speed, volume and switching between clips and live sources.

The dockable **Rules** pane combines ordered conditions and actions across objects and groups. Rules
can respond to field values and to pointer, keyboard and lifecycle events, then change visuals,
animate shader parameters, trigger sound or media, or control coordinated playout.

## Shared design and coordinated playout

Template Sets share fonts, resources and component sources. Master Brand Kits add inherited tokens,
named typography presets and background styles, with overrides you can see and reset. Smart layout
modifiers fit text, attach backings, hide empty content and arrange grids or rings.

The **Transitions** pane coordinates lanes, template replacement, shared-object morphing and
make-room resizing. In-place data updates keep the current on-air scene without retaking it.

## Previews and integrations

Single and coordinated exports include a self-contained preview page with Studio-style controls,
exposed data and custom actions. A small web-player module hosts trusted exported graphics on
tutorial websites, and it's what plays the [examples](/examples) on this site. Exported pages can also
connect exposed fields and named live inputs to existing Nodos ElectroNode slots.

## Before you upgrade

Timeline Play now runs straight through by default. Turn on **Stop at Steps** to rehearse playout;
this doesn't change how an exported graphic plays. Compact template sets use archive schema 6, so
save a compatible schema 5 copy if an older Studio has to read them.
