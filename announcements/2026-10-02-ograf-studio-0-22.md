OGraf Studio 0.22 is out. Shaders can now process a layer as well as fill it, and more of a
graphic's typography can follow operator data.

<!-- truncate -->

## Shaders as effects

Add **Shader** to a layer's **Effects stack**, and it runs as a true post-process pass: `iChannel0`
is the layer as it looks after the effects above it. Its output blends into the stack like any
other effect, so you can reorder, bypass and duplicate it alongside blur, glow and colour
adjustments.

Shader fills gain a portable **Image input**: one PNG or JPEG, sampled as `iChannel0`, with Repeat
or Clamp wrapping and Linear or Nearest filtering. Exposed shader sliders now move smoothly while
you drag them.

## Data bindings for typography

Text content and typography properties can be bound to data fields. For **Select** fields,
**Advanced mapping** gives each option its own value, so a single choice can switch a font family,
size or line height.
