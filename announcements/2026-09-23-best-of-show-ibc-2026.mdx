OGraf Studio is an open-source, browser-based editor for EBU OGraf graphics. Designers build lower
thirds, scoreboards, tickers and full-screen graphics visually or with AI, connect them to operator
data, and export portable HTML5 packages that are checked before they're written.

Thank you to everyone who has tried Studio, reported issues and contributed so far.
